What's next

The forward-looking enterprise

Where the next 18–36 months are heading, based on current trajectories.

Enterprise-wide

Adoption goes horizontal

AI stops living in pockets. By the end of 2026, ~40% of enterprise apps ship with built-in agents — finance, ops, HR and the front office adopt in parallel, not in sequence.

ROI-led

From cost story to value story

The mandate shifts from "deploy AI" to "prove EBIT impact." Spend concentrates on a few high-return use cases, with returns averaging $3–4 per dollar where execution is disciplined.

Agentic

Agents become the interface

Agent-software spend roughly doubles to $376B in 2027. By 2035, agentic AI could drive ~30% of enterprise-application revenue — north of $450B.

Governed

Trust becomes the moat

As agents take real action, governance, data quality and human-in-the-loop controls separate the scalers from the cancelled projects. Oversight is the new differentiator.
